This mediates production and decay channels such as \(W^\ast\to \ell N_1\), \(N_1\to \ell W\), \(N_1\to \nu Z\), and \(N_1\to \nu H\), with Majorana self-conjugacy allowing lepton-number-violating charged-lepton signatures.
